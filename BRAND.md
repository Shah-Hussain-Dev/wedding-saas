## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Positioning

### 1.1 Brand Identity Overview
**WedInvites** is a luxury digital invitation atelier and SaaS platform that elevates traditional South Asian and multicultural wedding stationery into interactive, cinematic 3D web experiences. 

Instead of static, discarded paper cards or flat PDF files, WedInvites delivers living digital wedding invitations directly to guests' phones via WhatsApp. Every invitation features 3D carved palace door reveals, animated wax seals, scratch-to-reveal wedding dates, synchronized classical acoustic soundscapes (sitar, shehnai, violin), 1-click Google Maps venue directions, and automated real-time RSVP tracking.

### 1.2 Core Brand Attributes
* **Brand Name:** `WedInvites` (Stylized in lowercase brand mark: `wedinvites` with a gold accent gem `•`, or Title Case in editorial text: `WedInvites`)
* **Primary Domain:** `wedinvites.in`
* **Industry / Niche:** Wedding Tech, Luxury Digital Stationery, Interactive Web Experiences, Event SaaS.
* **Geographic Focus:** India, UAE, UK, US, Canada, Australia (South Asian Diaspora, NRI, and Global Modern Couples).
* **Price Point:** **₹1,199** flat all-inclusive lifetime pass (introductory rate slashed from ₹2,999) — zero per-guest fees, unlimited edits, lifetime hosting.

### 1.3 Brand Slogans & Manifestos
* **Primary Slogan:** *"Not a card. An experience."*
* **Hero Hook:** *"Make the invitation as unforgettable as the day."*
* **Value Slogan:** *"One simple price. Infinite celebration."*
* **Design Philosophy:** *"The Architecture of Enchantment — Modern craft, heritage roots."*
* **Operational Hook:** *"Zero courier delays. 1-click WhatsApp delivery. 3D living art in your guests' hands."*

### 1.4 Brand Archetype & Tone of Voice
* **Brand Archetype:** **The Royal Couturier meets Modern Visionary** (*Lover + Creator*).
* **Tone of Voice:**
  * **Regal yet Approachable:** Uses elevated, poetic language ("sacred union", "starlit terrace", "24K gold foil", "masterwork") without sounding pretentious or stiff.
  * **Emotionally Resonant:** Celebrates family legacy, cultural heritage, and modern couple romance.
  * **Confident & Tactile:** Emphasizes sensory details — embossed ivory paper, velvet weight, molten wax seals, sitar harmonics.
  * **Relief & Empowerment:** Alleviates the severe stress, printing delays, and shipping costs of traditional physical wedding cards.

## 3. Logo & Favicon Specifications

### 3.1 Logo Architecture
1. **The Wordmark:**
   * Clean lower-case serif: `wedinvites` set in high-contrast Cormorant Garamond / Didot styling.
   * Accompanied by a 24K gold geometric dot (`#C8A45E`) representing the auspicious *bindi / tilak / seal of perfection*.
2. **The Icon / Monogram:**
   * An interlocking dual-letter emblem: A royal calligraphic **`W`** whose central strokes intertwine to form **two overlapping wedding rings**, topped with an architectural Mughal/Rajput pointed arch or a wax seal border.
   * Alternative: A round **Molten Wax Seal** debossed with an ornate serif monogram `W` accented with twin lotus petals or celestial stars.

### 3.2 Favicon Specs
* **Shape:** Rounded squircle (iOS icon standard) or circular wax seal emblem.
* **Colors:** Deep Imperial Emerald (`#073D31`) background with a high-contrast 24K polished Champagne Gold (`#C8A45E`) monogram.
* **Sizes to generate:** 16×16, 32×32, 48×48, 180×180 (Apple Touch Icon), 512×512 (PWA / Web App Manifest).

## 7. Creative Guidelines & Brand Guardrails

### 7.1 What WedInvites IS:
* **Cinematic & Tactile:** Feels like touchable luxury (gold foil, rich velvet, carved wood, textured linen).
* **Culturally Reverent:** Authentic respectful representation of Hindu, Muslim, Sikh, and universal intercultural traditions.
* **Mobile-First Masterpiece:** Designed ergonomically for thumb interaction on 360px–430px smartphone screens.
* **Facial Clearance Guarantee:** In all marketing and templates, couple faces and expressions are never covered by floating badges or banners.

### 7.2 What WedInvites IS NOT:
* **NOT a PDF or static graphic flyer:** Never describe it as an "e-card" or "image invite". It is an **interactive web experience** or **digital portal**.
* **NOT cheap or disposable:** Even at an affordable ₹1,199, the aesthetic must always reflect five-star luxury.
* **NO generic tech clichés:** Avoid cold robotic blue gradients, neon cyan, or sterile corporate aesthetics. The colors must always remain warm, rich, and regal.
